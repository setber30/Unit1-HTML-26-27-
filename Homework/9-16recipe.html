Chocolate Chip Cookies

I chose Chocolate Chip Cookies because they are a simple and tasty treat.

Navigation

  • Ingredients
  • Steps
  • Notes

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Salt
  • Chocolate Chips
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Butter
  • Baking Soda
  • Vanilla Extract

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 375 degrees farenheit
  2. Whisk flour, salt, and baking soda together
  3. Mix the butter and sugar in a bowl
  4. Add eggs into bowl and beat until well blended then mix in vanilla. Add the dry ingredients into the mixture. Fold in chocolate.
  5. Put small scoops of the mixture on a baking sheet and bake for 8-10 minutes.
  6. Enjoy

Notes

  • Equipment
    • Whisker
    • Oven
  • Substitutions
    • Can remove chocolate altogether
    • Honey instead of sugar
